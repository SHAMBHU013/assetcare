3. Google AdMob & Third-Party Advertising

To provide AssetCare free of charge to homeowners worldwide, the application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), an advertising service operated by Google LLC (“Google”).

Ad Transparency: Google AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (AAID), IP address (for general country/city level location), and ad engagement metrics to deliver relevant banner and interstitial ads and prevent click fraud.

How to Control or Opt Out of Personalized Ads:

You have full control over advertising personalization directly through your Android device settings and third-party tools:

  • Reset or Delete Android Advertising ID: On your Android device, navigate to Settings > Google > Ads and select “Reset advertising ID” or “Delete advertising ID”.
  • Google Ad Settings: You can manage ad personalization by visiting Google’s official portal at https://adssettings.google.com.
  • In-App European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Consent: In applicable jurisdictions, AssetCare displays the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can change your consent preferences at any time under Settings > Ad Privacy & Consent Choices inside the app.
  • Industry Opt-Out Platforms: You can opt out of interest-based ads from participating networks via the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA).
  • Ad-Free Experience (Pro Plan): Users who purchase the AssetCare Pro/Premium plan enjoy a 100% ad-free experience. All ad requests and ad-tracking SDK activities are disabled for premium users.

4. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

In compliance with the principle of least privilege and Google Play Developer Program policies, AssetCare requests only the minimum runtime and install-time permissions necessary:

  • android.permission.INTERNET (Normal permission):
    Allows the app to fetch advertisements via Google AdMob, verify subscription status with Google Play Billing, and check connectivity.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (Normal permission):
    Enables the app to monitor whether an active Wi-Fi or cellular network connection exists before attempting network calls.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Runtime permission on Android 13+):
    Requested strictly so that the app can alert you about upcoming warranty expirations, seasonal home maintenance tasks (e.g., HVAC filter changes, winterizing pipes), and scheduled contractor follow-ups. You can grant or revoke notification permissions at any time via Android system settings.

Permissions We Do NOT Request: AssetCare does not request access to your device location (GPS/Coarse), microphone, contacts, calendar, phone state, SMS, or biometric data.

5. Photos & Document Attachments

Homeowners frequently attach photos of work done, receipts, or warranty stickers to their repair records. AssetCare complies strictly with Google Play’s storage policies:

  • No Broad Storage Permissions: We do not request or utilize broad permissions such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES.
  • Android Photo Picker: All images are selected using the official zero-permission Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). The app only receives access to the specific images you explicitly choose.
  • App-Private Storage: Selected images are copied directly to the app’s private, sandboxed internal storage directory on your device. Other apps cannot view or access these files.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because your data belongs to you, you maintain complete sovereignty over your information at all times:

  • In-App One-Click Data Wipe: You can purge all property records, service visits, contractor details, warranty logs, and attached photos at any time by going to Settings > Delete All Service Data.
  • Export & Portability: You can export your service records to universal .json or .csv formats via Settings > Export & Backup Data before clearing your device.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling AssetCare from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes all SQLite database records and locally saved photo attachments stored in the app’s private container.
  • Right of Erasure: To request assistance with data inquiries or verify that no personal data resides on our external systems, please email us at the contact address below.

9. International Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

For European Economic Area (EEA) and UK Users (GDPR):

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you possess rights including:

  • Right of Access: Confirm whether your personal data is processed and request a copy.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete records directly within the app.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Delete your records via the in-app reset button or by uninstalling the application.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Revoke consent for personalized advertising through the Google UMP consent form or Android settings.

For California Residents (CCPA / CPRA):

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal data categories are collected and how they are used.
  • Request deletion of their personal information.
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (including cross-context behavioral advertising via advertising identifiers).
  • Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

Notice of Non-Sale: Appnexa does not sell your personal home, service, or financial data to data brokers or third parties.

10. Data Security Standards

We employ comprehensive technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information:

  • Encrypted Sandboxing: Android OS application sandboxing prevents other installed apps from accessing AssetCare’s local SQLite database and photo directory.
  • Transport Layer Security: Any network traffic initiated by Google AdMob or Google Play Billing is strictly encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • No Plaintext Telemetry: We do not log home addresses, warranty values, or phone numbers to external remote analytics servers.
